# Household energy statement 2025

Prepared for: the Kovács household, Budapest (3-bedroom flat, 4 residents)

## Electricity

Our household used 3,120 kWh of electricity in 2025 according to the annual meter reading.

In 2024 the same meter recorded 3,480 kWh, so consumption fell by 360 kWh year on year.

The regulated residential tariff we paid was 36 HUF per kWh up to the annual cap, and the
share above the cap was billed at the market rate.

## Heating

Gas use for heating and hot water was 1,050 cubic metres in 2025.

## Notes

The drop in electricity use followed replacing the refrigerator and the tumble dryer in March 2025.
