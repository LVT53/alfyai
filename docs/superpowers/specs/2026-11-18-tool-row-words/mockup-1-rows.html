Tool rows, opened

Each card shows one row as it is today and as I'd propose it. Click a row to open or close it, the way it works in the chat. The chrome here is English; the row language switch changes only what a person would read in the chat.

The rule we agreed
  • Opening a row gives a little more than the tool's name: the things it found or touched, by their own names, in plain words.
  • Never the model's side: no argument names, no ids, no note written for the model.
  • A failure says why, once.
  • A stop the app made on purpose isn't a red failure.
  • And one of mine: a row with nothing more to say doesn't open (no chevron).
