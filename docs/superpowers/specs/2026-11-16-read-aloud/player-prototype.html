A sourdough starter needs three things: flour, water and time. Mix equal weights of flour and water in a clean jar, cover it loosely, and leave it somewhere warm. For the first few days nothing much seems to happen, and that is normal.

Feed it once a day at room temperature, or once a week if it lives in the fridge. Each feed means discarding about half and adding fresh flour and water. When it doubles within six hours of a feed, it is ready to bake with.

Wie sagt man das auf Deutsch?

Ein Sauerteigansatz braucht drei Dinge: Mehl, Wasser und Zeit. Füttere ihn einmal am Tag, dann ist er nach etwa einer Woche bereit.

And how do I know it has gone bad?

Pink, orange or fuzzy spots mean mould, and then it is time to start again. A sharp smell like nail polish only means it is very hungry. Feed it twice in a day and the smell goes away.
