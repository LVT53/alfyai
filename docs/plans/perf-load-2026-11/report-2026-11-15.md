Production build served by the e2e harness at http://127.0.0.1:5175; commit `0b9138817`. Node v22.23.4. Generated 2026-11-15T13:46:26.438Z.

This is a measurement run, not a gate: the harness is inert unless `PERF_RUN=1` and it asserts nothing about the numbers.

## Method

- Network: CDP `Network.emulateNetworkConditions` — 400 ms latency, 51200 B/s download, 51200 B/s upload, `cellular3g`; CPU: `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate` at 4.
- Cache: cold = a fresh browser context with Network.clearBrowserCache before the navigation; warm = the same context and page as the cold landing, reloaded with its cache kept
- Sign-in: POST /api/auth/login through the browser context's request client (not a page visit), so no app asset was fetched into the cache before the measured navigation
- Usable-at: `page.waitForSelector(visible)` on `[data-testid="message-input"]` followed by a real `locator.click()`; the value is `performance.now()` read one CDP round trip after the click, so it is an approximation that includes Playwright's actionability checks and the click itself. A click can land on server-rendered markup before hydration; the framework's own hydration marker (`#svelte-announcer`) is recorded separately as `hydratedAtMs`.
- FCP/LCP/CLS come from buffered `PerformanceObserver` entries installed before the document's scripts; long tasks from `longtask`, with TBT approximated as Σ max(0, duration − 50 ms).
- First appearance by element: the same pre-document recorder also runs a buffered `MutationObserver` that stamps the first `performance.now()` at which each landing marker exists and is visible — a non-zero box and a computed `display` other than `none`, so an element in the DOM but not on screen does not count. The markers are the greeting, the weekly bars, the projects container, the first recent row and the composer. The values are each landing scenario's `elements` array in the raw file (sorted by arrival) and its "First appearance by element" table below. A marker that never became visible is `null` / `n/a`; the client-side transition's arrivals are on the landing document's clock, like `chatSurfaceAtMs`.
- CLS is reported twice: `cls.total` sums every layout-shift value, `cls.excludingRecentInput` drops the shifts the browser attributes to recent input.
- LCP observation ends at the first trusted input, and the usable-at step is a real click: the LCP value below is the largest contentful paint observed up to that click, not past it. `paints.firstInputAtMs` in the raw file says when that click happened.
- A property of the measurement stack, stated so TTFB is not misread: with this CDP emulation the configured latency is applied between `responseStart` and `responseEnd`, not before `responseStart`. A standalone probe with 2 s of emulated latency made a `fetch` take 2011 ms and the document commit at 2021 ms while the resource entries reported `responseStart` about 1 ms after `requestStart`. The requested TTFB formula below therefore excludes the emulated round trip; the `Document response (requestStart → responseEnd)` row shows where that latency appears, and FCP, LCP, load and the usable numbers include it.
- Resources: every entry from `performance.getEntriesByType("resource")` (name, initiatorType, startTime, duration, transferSize, encodedBodySize, decodedBodySize, responseStart, responseEnd). Each resource is classified by file extension first (js/mjs = script, css = stylesheet, woff/woff2/ttf/otf/eot = font, png/jpg/jpeg/webp/avif/gif/svg/ico/bmp = image), then by initiatorType (script, img/image, fetch/xmlhttprequest), and everything else is other.
- The HTML document is not a resource entry: its own bytes are in the navigation entry (`transferSize`, `encodedBodySize`, `decodedBodySize`), and request counts and byte sums below exclude it.

## Observations

Every scenario ran over a 400 ms-RTT cellular3g profile at 50 KB/s down and up with the CPU throttled ×4; a "cold" run started from a fresh browser context with its cache cleared, and the warm run reloaded the cold landing context with its cache kept. The cold desktop landing painted FCP at 14696 ms and LCP at 14696 ms, reached DOMContentLoaded at 14695.9 ms and load at 32197.6 ms, and its composer accepted a click at 31435.5 ms; it made 101 subresource requests for 1941.6 KB, of which script was 1567.3 KB (80.7%), fonts 127.5 KB (6.6%), stylesheets 232.1 KB and fetch/xhr 14.7 KB, in 76 distinct script chunks whose last response ended at 40897 ms. On the cold desktop landing, the sections first became visible in this order: greeting at 894.4 ms, composer at 913.1 ms (+18.7 ms after the previous section), weekly-bars at 41496.7 ms (+40583.6 ms after the previous section), recent-first-row at 41496.7 ms; projects never became visible. The phone landing (390 × 844) made 100 requests for 1941.2 KB (1 fewer request and 0.3 KB less than the desktop run), with FCP at 14700 ms against 14696 ms, and its composer accepted a click at 31437.1 ms. The warm reload of the same landing page made 100 requests for 15.2 KB (0.8% of the cold transfer), of which 87 requests reported a transfer size of 0, and its composer accepted a click at 1403.3 ms with FCP at 896 ms. The cold chat load of a seeded eight-message conversation made 135 requests for 2784.9 KB (script 2199.1 KB, fonts 127.5 KB), in 103 distinct script chunks whose last response ended at 60173.5 ms, with FCP at 20488 ms, LCP at 24904 ms, DOMContentLoaded at 20485.5 ms, load at 43297.7 ms and its composer clickable at 35952.9 ms. From the landing page, clicking the conversation reached the chat URL after 18185.4 ms, attached the chat surface after 18204.8 ms and a clickable composer after 18241.6 ms, adding 46 subresource requests for 853.9 KB with no new document, paint entry or navigation timing entry. Across the measured loads the three resources contributing the most bytes were 1) `/_app/immutable/chunks/D_GezWJJ.js` (script) at 632.3 KB, 2) `/_app/immutable/nodes/5.C96uVmIq.js` (script) at 374.2 KB, 3) `/_app/immutable/entry/app.BevTs-W3.js` (script) at 228.1 KB. The framework's hydration marker was recorded at 41271.2 ms on landing-cold-desktop (composer clickable at 31435.5 ms); 41272.1 ms on landing-cold-phone (composer clickable at 31437.1 ms); 1409.2 ms on landing-warm-reload (composer clickable at 1403.3 ms); 59362.6 ms on chat-cold (composer clickable at 35952.9 ms), so on the cold documents the first click landed on server-rendered markup before the app mounted. The landing document the transition started from had hydrated at 41223.1 ms (landing navigation start), before the conversation was clicked.
