This is a measurement run, not a gate: the harness is inert unless `PERF_RUN=1` and it asserts nothing about the numbers.

## Method

- Network: CDP `Network.emulateNetworkConditions` — 400 ms latency, 51200 B/s download, 51200 B/s upload, `cellular3g`; CPU: `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate` at 4.
- Cache: cold = a fresh browser context with Network.clearBrowserCache before the navigation; warm = the same context and page as the cold landing, reloaded with its cache kept
- Sign-in: POST /api/auth/login through the browser context's request client (not a page visit), so no app asset was fetched into the cache before the measured navigation
- Usable-at: `page.waitForSelector(visible)` on `[data-testid="message-input"]` followed by a real `locator.click()`; the value is `performance.now()` read one CDP round trip after the click, so it is an approximation that includes Playwright's actionability checks and the click itself. A click can land on server-rendered markup before hydration; the framework's own hydration marker (`#svelte-announcer`) is recorded separately as `hydratedAtMs`.
- FCP/LCP/CLS come from buffered `PerformanceObserver` entries installed before the document's scripts; long tasks from `longtask`, with TBT approximated as Σ max(0, duration − 50 ms).
- CLS is reported twice: `cls.total` sums every layout-shift value, `cls.excludingRecentInput` drops the shifts the browser attributes to recent input.
- LCP observation ends at the first trusted input, and the usable-at step is a real click: the LCP value below is the largest contentful paint observed up to that click, not past it. `paints.firstInputAtMs` in the raw file says when that click happened.
- A property of the measurement stack, stated so TTFB is not misread: with this CDP emulation the configured latency is applied between `responseStart` and `responseEnd`, not before `responseStart`. A standalone probe with 2 s of emulated latency made a `fetch` take 2011 ms and the document commit at 2021 ms while the resource entries reported `responseStart` about 1 ms after `requestStart`. The requested TTFB formula below therefore excludes the emulated round trip; the `Document response (requestStart → responseEnd)` row shows where that latency appears, and FCP, LCP, load and the usable numbers include it.
- Resources: every entry from `performance.getEntriesByType("resource")` (name, initiatorType, startTime, duration, transferSize, encodedBodySize, decodedBodySize, responseStart, responseEnd). Each resource is classified by file extension first (js/mjs = script, css = stylesheet, woff/woff2/ttf/otf/eot = font, png/jpg/jpeg/webp/avif/gif/svg/ico/bmp = image), then by initiatorType (script, img/image, fetch/xmlhttprequest), and everything else is other.
- The HTML document is not a resource entry: its own bytes are in the navigation entry (`transferSize`, `encodedBodySize`, `decodedBodySize`), and request counts and byte sums below exclude it.

## Observations

Every scenario ran over a 400 ms-RTT cellular3g profile at 50 KB/s down and up with the CPU throttled ×4; a "cold" run started from a fresh browser context with its cache cleared, and the warm run reloaded the cold landing context with its cache kept. The cold desktop landing painted FCP at 14584 ms and LCP at 14584 ms, reached DOMContentLoaded at 14583.4 ms and load at 20569.1 ms, and its composer accepted a click at 31465.4 ms; it made 101 subresource requests for 1937.6 KB, of which script was 1563.4 KB (80.7%), fonts 127.5 KB (6.6%), stylesheets 232.1 KB and fetch/xhr 14.7 KB, in 76 distinct script chunks whose last response ended at 40813.1 ms. The phone landing (390 × 844) made 100 requests for 1937.3 KB (1 fewer request and 0.3 KB less than the desktop run), with FCP at 14652 ms against 14584 ms, and its composer accepted a click at 30964.6 ms. The warm reload of the same landing page made 100 requests for 14.4 KB (0.7% of the cold transfer), of which 90 requests reported a transfer size of 0, and its composer accepted a click at 1410.8 ms with FCP at 864 ms. The cold chat load of a seeded eight-message conversation made 135 requests for 2769.3 KB (script 2228.7 KB, fonts 84.6 KB), in 104 distinct script chunks whose last response ended at 60318.2 ms, with FCP at 20348 ms, LCP at 25500 ms, DOMContentLoaded at 20346.4 ms, load at 25698.8 ms and its composer clickable at 36003 ms. From the landing page, clicking the conversation reached the chat URL after 18033.1 ms, attached the chat surface after 18057.5 ms and a clickable composer after 18094.1 ms, adding 46 subresource requests for 844.7 KB with no new document, paint entry or navigation timing entry. Across the measured loads the three resources contributing the most bytes were 1) `/_app/immutable/chunks/OxTN49w0.js` (script) at 631.6 KB, 2) `/_app/immutable/nodes/5.B9FeidP-.js` (script) at 368.4 KB, 3) `/_app/immutable/entry/app.Drj7vlvj.js` (script) at 228.1 KB. The framework's hydration marker was recorded at 41308.1 ms on landing-cold-desktop (composer clickable at 31465.4 ms); 41305.9 ms on landing-cold-phone (composer clickable at 30964.6 ms); 1414.7 ms on landing-warm-reload (composer clickable at 1410.8 ms); 58416.1 ms on chat-cold (composer clickable at 36003 ms), so on the cold documents the first click landed on server-rendered markup before the app mounted. The landing document the transition started from had hydrated at 41242.6 ms (landing navigation start), before the conversation was clicked.
