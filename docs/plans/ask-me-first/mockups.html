Ask me first

Alfy stops in the middle of a reply, asks you, and carries on from your answer in the same reply. Questions and answers are Alfy's own words, in the chat's language. The app adds only the small labels (section 8).

1 · Alfy stops and asks

The reply pauses where the question is. The words above it stay where they are, and the reply's own buttons (copy, retry) wait until it is finished. A question with one pick always has a Recommended answer, which Alfy marks. With one question and one pick, a click answers at once. The X closes the question without an answer. While it waits, the message box is off and what you type goes into the card's own line (section 4).

3 · After you answer, the same reply carries on

The card folds into one line, like a finished step, and your answer is not repeated as a message of its own. Alfy carries on below that line, still holding everything it found before it asked. The reply's buttons come back at the very end.

9 · What is decided

Yours on the left, from today's conversation. Mine on the right: change any of them.

Yours

  • After you answer, the same reply carries on, instead of a second round in a new reply.
  • A card holds 1 to 3 questions with 2 to 3 answers each and an optional one-line note. A question may take several picks. "Type my own" is always there, and "Up to you" lets Alfy decide.
  • A question with one pick always has a Recommended answer, and Alfy marks which one. On a question with several picks it is optional.
  • An X at the card's top right closes the question: it tells Alfy the question was off, or to guess.
  • Your own answer is typed in the card's own line, not the message box.
  • No timer: a question waits for you.
  • Once answered, the card folds into one line and cannot be changed. Retry runs the whole reply again.
  • A chat that waits for you shows a small icon before its title in the sidebar, with no words.
  • No limit on when or how often Alfy asks: before it makes something, at a fork in a conversation, or while you talk an idea through.

My calls

  • The paused reply is saved, and your answer or the X starts it again, from any device, at any time. Nothing stays connected while it waits.
  • After the X, Alfy hears that you closed the question without answering. It carries on with its best guess or asks differently, and does not ask the same thing again.
  • While a question waits, the message box is off. Your answer or the X turns it back on, and anything you had typed in it is still there.
  • The Recommended mark stays where Alfy put it, so an order like small, medium, large keeps its sense. On a question with several picks the mark may sit on several answers, or none.
  • "Up to you" stays, as in the design you approved. Where a question with several picks has no Recommended, it is how you leave the choice to Alfy.
  • One question with one pick answers on a click. Several questions, or a question with several picks, need Send.
  • Its answers take no tap for a moment after it appears, as on the permission card.
  • What Alfy does after your answer is drawn below the folded line, never above it: nothing may push the words being read.
  • Your answer shows in the folded line only, not as a message bubble.
  • The sidebar mark is a small question mark where the fork and incognito marks sit, not the dot a finished research run uses, so the two cannot be confused. A screen reader hears "Waiting for your answer".
  • A question has no short heading of its own: fewer words for Alfy to write and for the tool list to carry.
  • On a phone the card stays in the reply; no sheet.
  • "Up to you" and "Rád bízom" say your "Skip, you decide" in fewer words.
  • Not in this version: pictures as answers (which of these photos), changing an answer, and a phone notification when Alfy asks.
