Image generation · first wireframes

Low-fidelity screens for settling what the UI has to support. They are drawn in AlfyAI's tokens and reuse its panel, cards and History; every label is a first guess. The pictures follow your couch session: make a sofa, recolour it, put it into a real photo, move it.

A · Where it lives: three shapes

Settled on 26 Oct: B. The picture becomes a sixth kind in the family (Image / Kép), so it gets the panel, History, Download and the library that Documents and Canvases already have.

A · In the chat only

Each picture sits full size in a reply, and each change is a new reply.

  • Nothing new to learn
  • Comparing means scrolling
  • No room to mark a spot or to step back

B · A card in the chat, the picture in the panel CHOSEN

The chat shows each result as a card; the panel shows the current picture large, with marks and versions.

  • Big picture, versions and marks beside the chat
  • The same panel, History and library as the other kinds
  • One more kind for the family to carry

C · A separate Images page

A gallery with its own prompt bar and settings, away from the chat.

  • Good for browsing everything made
  • Leaves the chat, so Alfy loses the conversation
  • Many settings, against "few choices"
