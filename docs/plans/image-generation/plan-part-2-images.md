# Images (Part 2) Implementation Plan

> **Draft, 2026-10-27.** Waits on the owner's answers to the plan's own calls (below). They are folded into all three plans before anyone builds from them.

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** In any chat, Alfy makes a new picture from words and changes pictures round after round: a sixth artifact kind, Image / Kép, made through one model-facing tool on one shared ComfyUI running Qwen-Image 2.1 on GPU1, with the card, the panel, History, numbered pictures and marks the design describes.

**Architecture:** A new server folder, `src/lib/server/services/images/`, owns the ComfyUI client and its fake, the graph templates, the canvas and seed rules, the pixel guards, the picture files, the cards and their takes, and the job kind that renders a take. The `make_image` tool (`normal-chat-tools/make-image.ts`) turns the model's call into a take, waits up to 100 s while the job's progress keeps the stream alive, retries once on a clear miss, and shows the model the result through part 1's step-picture queue. Each take lands as a version of an Image item on the existing artifact backbone; the browser gets an image card, an image body for the panel and an image preview for History.

**Tech Stack:** SvelteKit with Svelte 5 runes, TypeScript, SQLite with hand-written Drizzle migrations, the job engine (`services/jobs`, ADR-0069), `ai` 6.0.193, sharp 0.35.5 (already a dependency), Node 22's built-in `WebSocket` client, vitest, Playwright, Lucide icons. On GPU1: ComfyUI with Qwen-Image 2.1.

**Spec:** `docs/plans/image-generation/design.md`. Part 2 is section 5; the owner's decisions are section 6, my calls section 7, what part 1 changed is "Decided during the build", what is left to the builder is section 8, the seams are section 9. Wireframes: `wireframes.html`, frames 1 to 7 (frame 8 lists the decisions). Part 2 builds on part 1 exactly as it stands on `feat/vision-part-1` at `1b71f7cdd`: its plan is `plan-part-1-vision.md`, its open items `part-1-release-checklist.md`, and its build ledger's rulings R1 to R22 (`.superpowers/sdd/plan-part-1-vision/progress.md` in that worktree) bind where this plan cites them.

## Global Constraints

Every task in all three plans includes these. A plan adds its own below its header.

- Every user-facing string (label, error, empty state, aria) exists in English and Hungarian; Hungarian is short and natural and fits a 360 px row. The design's first guesses are used as written: Kép, Másik (Another), Ez legyen (Use this), Előtte / Utána, Jelölés (Mark), Csatolás (Attach), Lát képeket.
- The word "Artifact" never reaches the UI; the kind is named through `artifacts.type.image` ("Image" / "Kép"). No version number outside History's fine print (ruling 76). History is the family's one History: no second undo or version system.
- Ruling 79: everything the app writes into a model request is ASCII English (labels, notes, guidance). File names and titles ride inside it as the person's data. Read `src/lib/server/services/chat-turn/AGENTS.md` before writing any text a model reads.
- Svelte 5 runes, Lucide icons only, tokens from `src/app.css`. The eight mark colours are data, not chrome: one constant shared by the panel and the server painter.
- Qwen-Image 2.1 run settings: 40 steps, CFG 1; a fresh random seed for every take, never the seed that made its input; references addressed `<image1>`, `<image2>`, the picture being changed first; PNG for every stored picture; a new picture defaults to 1248x832; an edit is never exactly 1024x1024; a change keeps its picture's shape at about 1 MP, up to about 4 MP when the words ask for print or wallpaper.
- One ComfyUI on GPU1 serves ai.dev and production (driver 580 or newer, torch 2.14.1+cu130, ComfyUI pinned to a commit at or after v0.41.3, comfy-kitchen 0.2.39, no xformers, Dynamic VRAM on, `--reserve-vram`, `--listen 127.0.0.1`). The "N ahead" a card shows comes from ComfyUI's own queue.
- One take per tool call; up to four takes per card in one turn; one retry per take on a clear miss, and only the better take is shown. A picture's tool call waits up to 100 s and the job's progress counts as activity for the stream's idle watchdog. A picture that lands after that gets no look and no retry. Nothing starts a turn by itself.
- The composer's Stop cancels a take that has not started rendering; a take that has started finishes onto its card, whose own Stop cancels it.
- No content checker, no storage quota, no size, shape, style or quality control, no make-a-picture switch, no per-person switch: every account may make pictures.
- Pictures per message: `IMAGE_REFERENCE_CAP`, admin-set, default 4, between 1 and 10.
- An upload is never modified. The first change of a photo starts an Image item whose first version is the photo. Restore makes an older version the one in use and deletes nothing.
- F16: what a turn made is drawn after what it said; nothing is inserted above prose that is still streaming.
- Runtime settings flow through `src/lib/server/env.ts` and `src/lib/server/config-store.ts`, with a row in `src/lib/config/admin-config-registry.ts` and a line in `README.md` and `.env.example`.
- Read each folder's AGENTS.md before changing code in it. The new folders `src/lib/server/services/images/` and `src/lib/components/artifacts/image/` get their own AGENTS.md, listed in the root AGENTS.md Folder Rules.
- Part 1 rulings that bind here: the model run asks `providerCanSeeImages` from `vision/provider-vision.ts`, never from `model-vision.ts` (Task 4 of part 1, R13); a picture that is not an uploaded artifact never rides `currentImages` and is never described into `image_descriptions` (R15); a result look goes through the turn's `StepImageQueue` and its eight-picture budget (R17); `buildCurrentUserMessage` stays the one maker of picture parts in the current message; a failover attempt on a model that cannot see gets no picture parts. So one request still carries at most 16 pictures (8 in the message, 8 looks), the vLLM limit part 1's release checklist asks for.
- Fallow: no fifth circular dependency. An export a later task consumes may show as a finding until that task lands (R8); the final gate of each plan shows none beyond the baseline's four cycles.
- Commands: every npm, npx, vitest, svelte-check, build and Playwright command runs with the literal prefix `env PATH=/opt/homebrew/opt/node@22/bin:/opt/homebrew/bin:/usr/bin:/bin:/usr/sbin:/sbin` (R5), with literal paths and no `$VAR` (R4). The tasks write `npx …` for short; add the prefix when running. Never run `npm run check` or `npm test` while `npm run build` runs. No new dependency is planned; any that becomes necessary is pinned exactly and named in the task's report.
- Docs check before code: Svelte 5 and SvelteKit (MCP docs), Drizzle, Vitest, Playwright, the AI SDK 6 tool and step APIs, sharp, and ComfyUI's server routes (`docs.comfy.org`, "Server routes"). Each task report names the docs it checked.
- Migrations are hand-written and expand-only. Before writing one, read the newest number in `drizzle/` and the last `idx` of `drizzle/meta/_journal.json` (part 1 took `1777140000119`, idx 132; dev may have moved).
- Commit in small, focused commits with messages that say why. Nothing is pushed, merged or deployed without the owner's explicit request. Ask the owner before anything that reads a server's database or box.

## The plan's own calls

Design section 8 leaves these to the builder, and a few gaps needed a decision to write exact tasks. Each is marked "Plan's call" where it is used.

1. **The tool and its words.** One tool, `make_image`, for new pictures and changes. Its description is 303 characters, 76 estimated tokens at the test's 4.0 characters a token; the detail rides in the parameter descriptions, which the ceiling test does not count. It is paid for by a measured raise of `CATALOGUE_TOKEN_CEILING` from 5,222 to exactly the new total (about 5,298), with a dated comment (2a Task 12). The alternative, cutting 76 tokens from other tools, would change tools whose wording was tuned against real-model runs.
2. **When a take becomes the version in use.** A take is held while its tool call still decides about it; when the call is done with it (or gives up waiting), the first good take of the card becomes the version in use; a take that lands later becomes it on landing. "Use this" moves it. A retry the model asks for (`retryOf`) on a take already in use adds a new version, so History keeps the missed one (2a Tasks 9 and 13).
3. **The clear-miss test.** Deterministic, in the app, from the change mask the guards compute anyway: less than 0.2% of the frame changed, or less than half of the change inside the person's marks. On such a miss the tool retries once inside the same call. A miss only eyes can see (the wrong object changed) is the chat model's: it sees each result and may call again with `retryOf`. No extra judging model call (2a Tasks 5 and 13).
4. **The pixel guards.** Alignment by a coarse-to-fine search over shift (up to 6% of the side) and scale (0.94 to 1.06) on edge images; the change mask from a blurred difference at 256 px wide, cleaned, dilated 2% for shadows and feathered 1.5%; more than 60% of the frame changed counts as a deliberate global change (no paste-back, and that version becomes the new anchor); every fourth chained change pastes onto the original instead of the version in use ("a re-render from the original", done by compositing, without replaying prompts). Every threshold is provisional until 2c Task 5 measures real renders (2a Task 5).
5. **Where pictures live.** Every input, take and marked copy is a PNG under `data/image-files/<conversationId>/` with a row in `image_files`; a card table groups a turn's takes for one picture; takes are the job rows. Pictures go with their chat: an Image item is never kept after its chat is deleted, even when another chat links to it (2a Tasks 6, 7, 8).
6. **Progress.** ComfyUI's WebSocket through Node 22's built-in client gives step progress; `/queue` and `/history` polled once a second stay the source of truth, so a dropped socket costs only the progress bar. No new dependency; the fake's WebSocket server is about sixty lines of RFC 6455 (2a Task 3).
7. **Which takes share a card.** One card per Image item per turn; for a new picture, identical calls in one turn share one card (that is how "show me four" works: four identical calls). There is no "take of" parameter (2a Task 12).
8. **Marks.** Up to eight boxes, letters A to H with fixed colours (A red `#E53935`, B blue `#1E88E5`, C green `#43A047`, D orange `#FB8C00`, E purple `#8E24AA`, F cyan `#00ACC1`, G pink `#D81B60`, H brown `#6D4C41`). The server paints the copy from box fractions with letters drawn as strokes (no font on the box needed). The marks chip is an `image` chip, so the closed chip-kind list does not change (2a Task 11, 2b Task 6).
9. **The graphs.** 2a ships reference API graphs built from the Qwen-Image-Edit node set ComfyUI used in 2025, tested only against the fake; 2c Task 3 replaces them with graphs exported from the installed server's own Qwen-Image 2.1 templates, and a contract test checks every slot.
10. **One cap for two limits.** `IMAGE_REFERENCE_CAP` limits pictures attached to one message and pictures given to one call. It narrows part 1, which sent up to 8 attached pictures in any chat (2a Task 11, 2b Task 5).
11. **Smaller ones.** The Image kind gets no first-open tour. The card's "…" menu holds Download and History; Another and Attach are buttons. ComfyUI is told to free its memory after 15 idle minutes. The account archive leaves pictures out, with a note. The engine runs up to 6 image jobs per app (4 per person) so prompts reach ComfyUI at once and its queue is the one that orders them. A card holds at most 8 visible takes.

## Where the design was read one way

- "Each attached picture gets a number … matching `<imageN>`" and "references addressed as `<image1>`, … with the photo being changed first" cannot both hold when the scene is not the first picture ("put the sofa from 1 … into our room, 3"). The plan numbers pictures 1 to N for the person and the chat model, and the tool's own `pictures` order defines `<imageN>` for Qwen.
- Section 5.9 puts "checking and retrying" in the activity row; wireframe frame 7 draws it as a card state. The plan follows the design: the tool row says "retried once" and the card shows only the better take.
- Section 5.8 says Qwen can make transparent pictures; how ComfyUI asks Qwen-Image 2.1 for RGBA is unverified. 2c Task 3 finds out; until a transparent graph exists, a transparent request makes an ordinary picture and the model is told so.
- "Every four or five rounds, a re-render from the original" is read as re-anchoring by compositing onto the original (Plan's call 4): a true re-render would need the chained prompts replayed and could lose changes the person accepted.
- The acceptance thresholds are LPIPS, a neural metric. The app does not compute it; 2c measures it offline on the box.
- The degraded-tools banner shows only to admins today; other accounts learn that pictures cannot be made from the card's own words.
