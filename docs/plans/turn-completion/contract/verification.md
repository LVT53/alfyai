- [Selected file-linkage contract](durable-file-linkage.md): only exact current-occurrence trusted raw producer job IDs authorize association, not start/late snapshots. Critical linkage/readback precedes terminal receipt in both transports; heavy stream projections stay deferred. Separate `normalChatFinalization` failure preserves saved identity/accounting without rewriting model completion, priced attempts or raw answer. Persistence-only failure offers no generation Retry or automatic queue replay; secondary storage failure makes no false durability claim.
- Ledger/billing writes reconcile idempotently by stable identity subject to storage availability. Failed/unsaved known costs count once; unknown usage/cost and unavailable attempt outcome/provider finish facts stay nullable/unknown. Incognito tracking and read-time privacy policy follow their actual owners; legacy receipts remain supported.
- Missing output cap resolves before preparation through selected/provider settings, otherwise application reserve `C - deriveDefaultTargetConstructedContext(C)` (820 at 8192; 26215 at 262144). - Complete policy-gated context must fit the frozen reserve. No truncation, digest substitution, refetch, unsafe destination projection or cap reduction to force a smaller destination. Generic no-policy callers remain legacy.
- Finite real-model cases do not guarantee semantic correctness, topic/format compliance, synthesis fidelity, every failure branch, durability during total DB outage, or permanent quality improvement.
