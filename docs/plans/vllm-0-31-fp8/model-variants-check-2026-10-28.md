# Model variants check: is there a newer or better uncensored Flash-Next for alfyroot? (2026-10-28)

Method: primary sources only. The public Hugging Face API without login (search, per-repo metadata, `tree` listings with LFS hashes and last-commit dates, `commits`, `discussions`), raw `README.md` and `config.json` files, the UGI Leaderboard CSV, PyPI JSON, and GitHub through `gh api` (tags, commits, compare, PRs, issues, files at tags). Nothing was booted, no weights were downloaded, the box was not touched. The search shapes returned 343 distinct Flash-Next repos; after dropping GGUF, MLX, EXL3, AWQ and GPTQ-style uploads 163 remained, and I fetched config and file sizes for the 106 whose names mark them as uncensored, abliterated, heretic, NVIDIA, Local Inference Lab (LIL), Swift or QAD builds, then read the cards (in full or the claim lines) of the 18 that matter. Gated repos answer 401 without login, so the OrcaRouter parent's card and commit list, the OrcaRouter NVFP4/FP8 cards and the ukisai Swift base could not be read. "Inferred" marks anything I did not read directly. All dates are UTC.

**Summary.** Stay on the deployed checkpoint today and plan one trial for after vLLM 0.33.0 ships. Nothing newer than Qwen3.8-Flash-Next exists as open weights: it is still Qwen's newest LLM on Hugging Face (created 2026-08-24, last weight commit 2026-08-26), everything Qwen published since 2026-09-01 is an image model, and the hosted "Qwen3.8-Flash" has no weights repo that I could find. The deployed repo has had no commit since 2026-09-17, and its parent OrcaRouter checkpoint has had no weight change since 2026-08-27 (README edits only; the last, a promo banner on 2026-10-27, is why every OrcaRouter repo shows that date). So there is no newer Orca revision to swap to. The one real upgrade is a different quantisation of the same base: NVIDIA's `nvidia/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-FP4-Local` (quantisation-aware distillation, 95.2 GiB, PLE stored as NVFP4, GPQA-D 91.5 vs 92.2 BF16 by NVIDIA's own run). By my arithmetic it would cut the pinned PLE from about 65 GiB to about 32 GiB and free about 6 GiB of GPU for KV. It is not uncensored, and stock vLLM cannot load it: 0.32.0 does not read an NVFP4 PLE table (it allocates it as BF16), and no stock release decodes its compressed scales (PR #60646 is open). The best-evidenced uncensored build of it is `lesj0610/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-FP4-Local-Abliterated` (2026-10-25): NVIDIA's bytes unchanged, abliteration applied at inference by a vLLM plugin that also carries the scale decoder. Its own table: refusal-phrase hits on 625 harmful prompts fall from 97.92% to 0.16%, MMLU 85.35% to 84.05%, GSM8K 96.10% to 96.60%. Caveats: thinking was off in those runs, English only, one author, a plugin three days old that was tested on sm_80 and never on SM120, and it needs vLLM >= 0.32.1rc0 (`v0.33.0rc1` was tagged today; no stable yet). Against the status quo there is also an independent report (the Strata author, 2026-10-10) that OrcaRouter's weight edit shortens thinking and degrades long agentic runs; that is one third party's small-sample measurement, so it argues for an A/B, not for a conclusion. Every other uncensored build is a same-layout sidegrade (no footprint or engine gain), needs the LIL Docker fork or SGLang, or is BF16-only or too large for the card. No candidate publishes a Hungarian test.

## 3. The deployed checkpoint, its parent, and how it was uncensored

- Deployed repo: created 2026-09-17T03:42:44Z, modified 2026-09-17T06:57:18Z, sha `f24d2b68ff2814f24455ae86717be276619b5664`, six commits, no discussions. Per-file last-commit dates are all 2026-09-17. 125.96 GiB in 220 files; `ple_embedding_dtype: float8_e4m3fn`, ModelOpt `NVFP4`, vision tower and one MTP layer present, no `auto_map`. Chat template, generation config, tokenizer config and preprocessor config have the same git blob ids as the Qwen base (checked against the tree listings). Its card says the work is "the quantization, compatible packaging and runtime testing, not additional fine-tuning or uncensoring", tested "with Pennyroyal's Flash-Next native-MTP/FR-Spec path" (SGLang fork), and "not a claim of new benchmark scores".
- Parent [orcarouter/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Uncensored](https://huggingface.co/orcarouter/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Uncensored): created 2026-08-26T11:12:24Z, gated (`auto`), BF16, 335.30 GiB, HEAD `da29941aee97` modified 2026-10-27T06:57:19Z. Its tree with `expand=true` (first page, 50 entries, 43 of the 131 shards) shows the shards, `config.json`, template and generation config last committed 2026-08-27T13:54:47Z ("Initial commit"), and `README.md` last committed 2026-10-27T06:57:19Z ("Span Orca Connect promo across both columns"). Diffing the tree at `e096800036ec` (the revision [beginning-ai](https://huggingface.co/beginning-ai/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Uncensored-NVFP4) cites on 2026-10-10) against `main`: 144 files each, only `README.md` differs (12,786 to 13,740 bytes), no weight hash changed. All 32 `orcarouter/*` repos show lastModified 2026-10-27 (the same promo edit, inferred). Conclusion: no newer Orca weights, and the 2026-09-17 NVFP4 conversion was made from the current ones.
- Method, from sources I could read (the parent's own card is gated): [beginning-ai](https://huggingface.co/beginning-ai/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Uncensored-NVFP4) diffed every tensor against the base and found OrcaRouter changed 149 of 1,658 tensors, all residual-stream writers: the output projections of the 36 linear-attention and 12 sparse-attention layers, the routed and shared expert `down_proj` of all 48 layers, the token embeddings, one PLE projection and 3 MTP tensors; the router, norms, vision tower, output head and the 47.7 GiB PLE table are byte-identical to the base. The Strata card says the same set. That is a refusal-direction weight edit in every layer.
- OrcaRouter's own evidence is one exchange in [discussion #1](https://huggingface.co/orcarouter/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Uncensored/discussions/1) (2026-08-29): after a user showed that the card's thinking-on base-model refusal figures were a scoring artifact (an opening-phrase classifier fooled by the chain of thought, plus a 3,072-token cap), OrcaRouter re-judged the final post-`</think>` answer: base about 100% refusal with thinking on (20 of 20 on an AdvBench-style set), edited model about 0%; the thinking-off table (base 64-100% to edited about 0-3%) "was always the reliable comparison". Rule-based classifier, small samples, self-reported. No KL divergence figure for the Orca edit exists in anything I could read.
- Independent report against the edit: the Strata author's withdrawal notice on [Maximilian228/...-Strata](https://huggingface.co/Maximilian228/OrcaRouter-Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Uncensored-NVFP4-GPTQ-Strata) (2026-10-10): on a multi-step Claude Code task (an interactive 3D page) the original model ran 175 steps and checked its work, while the Orca build stopped after 10-70 steps in 3 runs; teacher-forced over 7,869 positions of the original's thinking it raises the `</think>` probability by +0.12 nats on average; on one captured 72K-token request it thought 997 tokens against the original's 1,699. The author blames the edit reaching late layers and offers instead a runtime switch confined to layers 8-33 (`</think>` +0.02 nats; 0 refusals of 104 English and 0 of 30 Ukrainian held-out requests with thinking on), which exists only in their own Strata engine. Small samples, one task, one author; it is the only measurement of the deployed edit's agentic cost that I found.
- The licence field is disputed on the parent: the card says Apache-2.0, [discussion #3](https://huggingface.co/orcarouter/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Uncensored/discussions/3) (open) says the base is Qwen Community License 1.0. Our deployed repo carries the Qwen Community License file.

## 5. Uncensoring quality: what is measured, by whom

Refusal numbers depend on protocol and scorer; thinking-on and thinking-off behave differently on this model. The lflare card measures the angle between the two regimes' refusal directions at 67.4-76.7 degrees (capture noise 1.5-3.5 degrees) and says a rank-1 edit from one regime leaves 96% of the other (author measurement). That is why probes must be run with thinking on and off.
