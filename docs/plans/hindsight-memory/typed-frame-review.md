# Independent typed-frame review

2026-11-18. Read-only review of `final-feasibility/frames-fast.cjs` in the [source archive](research-source/README.md), [`frames-results.json`](final-feasibility/frames-results.json) and [`fresh-frames-results.json`](final-feasibility/fresh-frames-results.json). No code/prompt changes or new model calls. Reasoning-model results were not available to this review.

**Verdict: promising containment mechanism, not a settled production representation.** Separating ownership from colour prevents several previous collateral deletions, and code-rendered properties reduce sentence-level contamination. But the actual outputs still lose clear useful information, misread shopping as possession, and depend on unstable identities and model-written transition labels. Some defects are deterministic harness mistakes; fixing those cannot establish extraction quality or full parity.

All 30 cases have `accepted: true`. Here that means structural validation/selection succeeded, **not** that desired facts survived, attribution was correct, the action was fulfilled or a Hindsight workflow passed. These files test extraction/edit projection; they do not demonstrate persisted Hindsight consolidation, scoped recall or recovery.

## Deterministic defects worth fixing regardless of model

1. **Admit every assertion-bearing field.** `admitted()` filters properties only. Object ownership is rendered directly from entities based on the model's `status`; entity evidence receives no quote-span admission. Name/holder/possession are semantic claims, not harmless metadata. Normalize role identities separately from supported relationships and apply the same evidence eligibility to ownership as to properties. Mutable names must not become another way to retain removed attributes.
2. **Use provenance spans with source identity, not `indexOf` and a quote regex.** Current matching checks the first occurrence only, accepts very short evidence, recognizes only ASCII double quotes/backticks and an English start-of-string `Translate:`. It neither binds a claim to a particular occurrence nor reliably separates quoted content from a personal assertion. Deterministically check valid spans and their supplied provenance; identifying the semantic boundary still requires reliable input structure or language understanding. Do not add a growing list of translation phrases as a claimed solution.
3. **Render negation without inventing history.** `not_has` means, at most, “does not have.” “No longer has” needs a supported possession transition. Neither representation may convert “not bought” into “not owned.” Preserve distinct relation predicates when they carry different meaning.
4. **Bind edits to claim/source lineage, not just entity/key strings.** `apply()` overlays an old correction on every matching old value regardless of time and allows any nonempty `change` quote to bypass the overlay. The quote's presence is not proof of a later event. Require explicit revision/event ordering and targeted claim identities; treat an unresolvable entity/key remap as an unresolved action, not success. A UI edit can pass stable IDs directly instead of asking a second model to rediscover its target.
5. **Validate semantics the schema can actually enforce.** Check string IDs/keys, evidence spans, relationship references, correction replacement constraints and action mode. Report zero matched targets separately from an applied edit. Define scalar versus repeatable properties: hobby already appears twice under one entity/key, so uniqueness cannot simply be imposed globally. Closed enums cannot by themselves establish entailment or completeness.
6. **Separate mechanical acceptance from quality grading.** Preserve the full pre-filter extraction, exclusions, conflicts and matched action targets. Grade ownership, attribution, useful-detail retention, temporal correctness and each requested edit separately. Never turn these structural `accepted` flags into a success percentage.

These are principled corrections. They do not include fixture-specific synonyms, city/event keyword patches or model-generated guesses replacing missing facts.

## What remains model-dependent

Verbatim evidence establishes that words occurred, not that the output follows from them. The current validator cannot prove that a quote is autobiographical, a purchase negative is an ownership negative, a role/object identity is stable, every independent detail was extracted, or a transition concerns the targeted property. Short English canonical values also lose qualifiers: for example, a diagnosis being ruled out becomes `not gluten`, which is less precise than the source and should not be rendered as a stronger clinical claim.

Model confidence/status fields are not independent admission evidence. Withholding unsupported candidates is necessary, but the user also needs useful automatic recall; dropping omitted new facts is not an acceptable substitute for accurate memory. The observed omissions need measured improvement on fresh cases, not just tighter validation.

## Design consequence

The UI can safely be designed around evidence-backed items, targeted correction/removal, explicit pending/conflict/failure states, source inspection and consistent publication. **Do not freeze the typed-frame ontology, automatic-admission guarantee or full replacement contract from these results.** Enforceable source/claim identity and actual extraction quality remain unresolved.

Owning span checks, stable action targets, delivery and privacy fencing is ordinary integration work. Owning a general entity registry, semantic property ontology, multilingual temporal-event resolution, provenance admission and cross-source correction engine is a material part of a custom memory system. This prototype currently relies on that latter work; it should be stated plainly before calling Hindsight a low-maintenance replacement. A separate model comparison may improve extraction, but cannot retrospectively validate these fast-run outputs or remove the deterministic design defects above.
