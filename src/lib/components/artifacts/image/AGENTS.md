# Image Components

The picture card and panel body of the Image kind (design 5.3-5.10). It plugs into the family's shells (`ArtifactCard.svelte`, `document-workspace/DocumentWorkspace.svelte`, `history/`); it builds no parallel shell. Motion and feel are held to the Document kind's bar (`.superpowers/sdd/MOTION-PARITY.md`).

- **`image-card-view.ts` is the card's one view model**, pure TypeScript fed an `ImageCardView` (server read model) and the component's `$t` and clock. Which takes show (`visibleTakes`: only the server's held automatic retry (origin `auto_retry`) lives in its first take's slot; the model's own retry (origin `retry`) is an ordinary take with its own progress, failure and "Use this"), which one is shown (`shownTake`), the state of a take (`takeState`, with `now` passed in; "starting" shows after `STARTING_SHOWN_AFTER_MS`), the subtitle, the eta words and Before/After availability. Components do not re-derive these; `shouldPollImageCards` calls `hasActiveImageTakes` and `hasHeldImageTakes` (a held take is read again until its hold is let go or runs out).
- A take the explicit-picture filter kept back (`image_blocked`, `image_unchecked`) is its own state, `blocked` (`unchecked` true for the second), never `failed`: a quiet surface, a muted eye-off, `artifacts.image.card.blocked*` / `unchecked*` words, and Try again only when unchecked (a blocked take's seed would make the same picture; Another stays).
- `failed.unavailable` covers every "cannot reach the picture server" code (`image_server_unreachable`, `image_server_not_configured`), not one. A change reads as words: no "edited ... ago" and no version number (History's fine print only).
- Strings are `artifacts.image.*` in `src/lib/i18n/artifacts.ts`, English and Hungarian; the kind's name is `artifacts.type.image`, the word "Artifact" never reaches the UI. Hungarian "Try again" is "Újrapróbálom" (the retry-button rule in `artifacts.test.ts`).
- **The card** (plan 2b T2): `ArtifactCard` draws the head (`chrome="full"`, kind `image`) and loads `ImageCardBody.svelte` lazily, drawing head and body together; `ToolActivityRow` builds the head from `cardSubtitle` and never an "edited ... ago" line. The chat page answers the card's `ImageCardAction`s (`ImageCardActionHandler`) through `client/api/images.ts`. The picture box is reserved from the take's `box` and never changes height: the state and the picture are stacked layers that crossfade; while a take waits, starts up or is made the box shows only a calm shimmer (one layer for all three states, a band moved by `transform` on `--duration-shimmer` and `--ease-out`, colours `--shimmer-highlight` over a tint; a still tint under reduced motion), its state line is in the sr-only `image-card-status` live region (read out when the state changes) and the time left in the sr-only, not live, `image-card-status-detail` beside it, so a poll never re-announces it, and Stop and Use this share one slot of the actions row (`image-card-swap`, one grid cell), so Use this fades in where Stop fades out and never jumps (Task 11; the strip's tiles still on their way shimmer the same); failed, unavailable, stopped, deleted and checking again keep their words in the box; Before / After floats over the picture (a sliding thumb, transform only); "Use this" sits in the actions row after Another, so the right-aligned "…" never moves; Another is disabled while any take of the card is queued or running, or the one pressed is on its way (Task 17); on a narrow card Open › holds its row (faded out) until the picture is an item. Hover feedback in the card is instant (no colour transitions): only transform and opacity animate.
- `ImagePicture.svelte` is the one picture element (card, strip, "Made from", and later the panel and History): aspect-ratio box, `decoding="async"`, fade-in on load, a new picture laid over the old one until it has faded in, `srcset` for `variant: "full"` (thumbnail width from `thumbWidth`, shared with the server's `IMAGE_THUMB_LONG_EDGE`). Before / After: Before is fetched once After is shown (idle), the picture flips only once Before has loaded (the switch answers at once), and an opaque pair fades only Before, on top, over an After that stays at opacity 1 (a see-through pair crossfades). "See-through" is the file's `hasAlpha`, which `storeImageFile` sets only when the request may be see-through (the transparent graph, or a see-through base) and a pixel is meaningfully not opaque (`services/images/AGENTS.md`). Its load attachment reads state untracked: a cached picture is complete at once, and a tracked read there loops (`effect_update_depth_exceeded`, found by the motion check).
- **The panel** (plan 2b T3): `ImageBody.svelte` is the kind's body in `ARTIFACT_BODIES` inside `DocumentWorkspace` (the open, dock, expand and phone sheet are the shell's). The picture is fitted by CSS alone: an aspect-ratio box sized with container units (`min(100cqw, 100cqh * ratio, natural width)`) on a `container-type: size` stage, never `object-fit: contain` around it and never a ResizeObserver, so the dock's width animation never re-flows it and the marks (T6) live in the picture's own rectangle. The panel loads the full picture (`ImagePicture` `variant: "original"`); the strip loads thumbnails. Under the picture is one row, reserved whatever it holds: the strip, then the tools (Before / After; T6's Mark); on a phone, once a box is drawn, the way back to the composer takes a second row. A version landing never moves the picture, and with nothing above it the picture has the rest of the height in every shape (expanded included). The body follows the page's latest version for the item (`ArtifactBodyProps.versionNumber`, from `artifact-versions.ts`): when it rises it reads the item and History's first page again (F23). It registers Download (`/api/images/files/<id>?download=1` with the chat named) and `reload`.
- `VersionStrip.svelte`: one square thumbnail per History moment, oldest first, read from History's image moments and named in History's words (`moment-text.ts`, never a version number); a frame calls `onOpenHistory(versionId)` with its moment's own version, and `HistoryMode`'s `initialVersionId` selects the rail entry that holds that version (`rail-model.ts` `entryKeyForVersion`, F11). Never one per version: Alfy's changes written in a row fold into one moment (F4, its newer picture), and its frame opens History on that moment's own row, so a frame always opens on a row the reader can see, with the same picture. On a narrow row the current frame is scrolled whole into view (at once on first paint and under reduced motion) and a side that hides frames fades (`strip-scroll.ts`, the classes `fade-start` / `fade-end`).
- **History** (plan 2b T4): the Image is a kind of the family's History (`HISTORY_PREVIEWS.image`, so `kindHasHistory("image")`); the rail, the toolbar and Restore are the shell's. `ImageVersionPreview.svelte` shows the version's picture FULL SIZE (ruled, review M1: History's preview is a main view like the panel's; only the rail uses the 320 px thumbnail) and, when it is compared with another (the version before it, or now), `CompareWipe.svelte`: the one compared with left of the cut, this one right of it, each clipped to its own side (so a see-through picture shows the shared checkerboard, `checkerboard.css`, never the other picture), cut by a native range input stretched half a thumb past each edge (pointer, touch and keyboard are the browser's own; the thumb's centre is under the hand). Both cuts are written to `clip-path` (and the line's `transform`) once per animation frame, never through a transition. A preview that cannot read a version shows its error, never the picture before, and History offers no Restore on it. Marks show on the picture they were drawn on (its `baseFileId` is the one underneath), in `MARK_COLOURS`; the prompt sits in a closed `<details>`. The preview reports no changes, so no stepper. The rail's image moments carry a 40 px thumbnail read in the chat's scope (`MomentRail` `conversationId`, from `HistoryMode`, F25). A picture's versions are named in its own words (`moment-text.ts`: "Alfy made the picture", "Your photo"). `images-history.spec.ts` holds the strip-to-History path (F11) and Restore; the motion check's `t4-*` flows put History open and Restore beside the Document's.
- `pinch-zoom.ts`: `pinchZoom` (an action, on the stage, phone only): two fingers zoom 1x-4x and pan, a double tap resets, another picture (`resetKey`, the file) starts at 1x, one finger and a mouse are left to the Mark tool. Only `transform` moves, written once per frame; `touch-action: none` is on the stage alone and a pinch's pointer events stop there. `zoomFrom` is the pure clamp.
- `BeforeAfterSwitch.svelte` is the one Before / After control (card and panel); `picture-tile.css` is the one tile look (card takes strip and panel version strip).
- `npm run check:image-chunks` holds `ImageCardBody` and `ImageBody` out of the chat route's first load (each fails on a static import); `tests/e2e/images-motion.spec.ts` is the motion check (card: videos, numbers and sheets go to `$MOTION_VIDEO_DIR` when it is set, `t2-*`; panel, side by side with the Document: `t3-*`; unset, nothing is recorded), and `artifact-panel-motion.spec.ts` runs the panel's item checks for an Image as for a Document.
- **Pictures in the composer** (plan 2b T5): Attach puts a picture in the chat's composer from the card's More menu (`ImageCardAction` `attach`), a strip frame (a corner dot, mouse only: on touch a frame opens History, where Attach is a 44 px button in `ImageVersionPreview`) and History. `onAttachPicture` threads page -> `DocumentWorkspace` -> `ImageBody` -> `VersionStrip` and -> `HistoryMode` -> preview (`HistoryPreviewProps`), beside History's own. The page holds the list (`composerPictures`); `chat/composer-pictures.ts` numbers uploaded photos and card pictures in one sequence in the order added (the server numbers the same way, `images/turn-pictures.ts`). Every Attach hands on when its picture was made (`at`: the take's or the version's time), so two pictures of one item are told apart in their chips: the older says when (`pictureChipNames`, "Sunset gradient · today 10:31"), never a version number. The composer has NO cap and says nothing about one (the cap limits one picture request on the server); the only bound is the server's `MAX_PICTURE_REFS = 64` abuse guard (F26): a contrived 65-ref message gets a 400. The send carries `pictureRefs` and `pictures` (names and numbers) so the optimistic bubble draws numbered thumbnails from its first paint; a queued turn put back restores its chips from `pictures`.
- **Marks** (plan 2b T6): `MarkLayer.svelte` lies in the panel picture's own rectangle (`.image-body-frame`, the box `pinch-zoom` transforms), so the boxes zoom and pan with the picture and every number is a fraction of it; `mark-geometry.ts` is the pure part (`boxFromDrag`, `nextLetter`, `hitMark`) and uses the parser's own `MIN_MARK_SIDE`, `MARK_LETTERS`, `MAX_MARKS` from `shared/artifacts/image-body.ts` (never a copy). With Mark on (a toolbar toggle, off while Before shows) one pointer draws: the box is written from the pointer event (no transition, lag 0), a press under 6 px is a tap that removes the last-drawn box under it, ANY other pointer that comes down anywhere (the layer, the stage's bands beside the picture, the page: a capture listener on the document) drops the box being drawn (the pinch is the zoom's), and a finger's drag is held by a non-passive `touchmove` listener in an action. While Mark is on, `pinchZoom`'s double tap does not reset the zoom (`doubleTapReset: false`): two quick taps there remove marks. Each box is a button ("Mark A, tap to remove"); a removed box fades on opacity on the ease-out token (`MOTION_EASE.out`, the function mirror of `--ease-out` in `utils/motion.ts`, `MOTION_DURATION.standard`). The page holds the marks with the file AND the item version they were drawn on (`ComposerMarks`), hands the body `marks` as an `ImageMarksRequest` (the body draws them only on that file and lets marks on any other file go), shows them as ONE `image` chip ("Marks A, B", the picture's thumbnail, the number the marked picture goes by: `markedPictureNumber`), and lets them go on a send, the chip's x, another item opening or a newer version of their item (`marksOutdated`, Review Focus 4). The send adds the marked picture as a ref when it is not attached already (F14); `markedPicture` (client-only, with the drawn-on `version`) brings the chip back on a restored turn, which a newer version then drops.
- **F14, widened (accepted, ledger):** the send adds the marked picture as a ref whenever it is not already attached, not only when it is not the open item's current file, so a queued turn that drains after the panel closed, or a version the page has not heard of, never gets `invalid_image_marks`; the server records it once (`openIsRef`) under the same number.
- **Marks on an uploaded photo**: `UploadPictureMarks.svelte` is the Mark tool for a `knowledge_artifact` photo in the panel (a composer tile, or a photo in a sent message; `DocumentPreviewRenderer` draws it only where the page takes marks). `MarkTools.svelte` and `MarkNote.svelte` are shared with `ImageBody`, so the phone's "Tell Alfy what to change" is one control. The page holds these marks through `ComposerMarks` with `uploadArtifactId`; looking at another photo keeps them (`marksOutdated`), drawing on another moves them with an Undo, and removing the photo's tile clears them. A photo the composer does not hold rides again with the send (`resentMarkedPhoto`), in `attachmentIds` and `attachments` both.
- Pictures load only from `/api/images/files/<id>` (`imageFileUrl`) with the chat's `conversationId`.
- **The first-open tour** (plan 2b T16) is the family's card (`artifacts/tour/`, see its AGENTS.md), not anything of this folder's: `"image"` is a shipped tour kind, the panel puts the card in the stage's row ahead of `ImageBody`, and nothing here carries an anchor for it. `artifact-panel-motion.spec.ts` runs its tour loop for the Image as for the Document, and `images-motion.spec.ts` records it beside the Document's (`t16-tour-*`).
- **Enlarge and thumbnails** (plan 2b T22): the app has ONE full-screen viewer, `chat/ImageLightbox.svelte` (a message's pictures, Canvas photos, and this kind); it zooms and pans (`chat/image-lightbox-zoom.ts`: fit is 1, 100% is `actualScale`, wheel/trackpad about the pointer, drag, double click or tap fit <-> 100% (2x where 100% is fit), + / - / 0, pinch through `pinch-zoom.ts`'s `zoomFrom` with the viewer's bounds, double tap through its `nextTap`), transform only, opening on the emphasis tokens and closing on ease-in with `|global` transitions. Every chat-page host loads it through `chat/image-lightbox-loader.ts` (`check:image-viewer-chunks` holds it out of the chat's first paint); the Canvas's `PhotoNode` imports it statically, inside the Canvas's own lazy chunk. Hosts keep it mounted once loaded and shut it by its index. A picture of known size holds its fitted box before the file arrives (`aspect-ratio` from `width`/`height`) and fades in on load; a finger's or a pen's double tap is the controller's `endTap` alone (the browser's following `dblclick` is the mouse's), and the panel enlarges only on a click whose press was a mouse's and did not move. `PictureViewer.svelte` is this kind's way in: the panel's Enlarge on the picture's corner (and a mouse click on the picture while Mark is off), the card's Enlarge (the card's picture still opens the panel); the full-size file, no marks. Lists show a picture's 320 px thumbnail in a reserved 40 px box through `PictureThumb.svelte` (the one thumbnail; `fill` for a gallery tile): `ArtifactCard` row `thumbnail` (from `DocumentWorkspaceItem.imagePreview`) and the Knowledge Base's rows and gallery (`KnowledgeDocumentItem.imagePreview`, from `imagePreviewOf` in `shared/artifacts/image-body.ts`, the one reading of a body's picture).
- **Galleries** (T22, T22b): `artifacts/ItemGrid.svelte` is the one gallery (tiles of title, type line, `PictureThumb` or the type's icon; columns from its own width by a container query, two at the narrowest), with `ui/ListGridToggle.svelte` and `utils/list-view-preference.ts` (per viewer, a key per list). The Knowledge Base offers it on the Images chip only (`knowledgeDocumentsView`); the panel's "What this chat made" offers it for every kind (`artifactPanelListView`).

Do not:

- add width/height/top/left animation; animate only transform and opacity, tokens only
- import image components statically into the chat route's first load (lazy, like the other bodies)
