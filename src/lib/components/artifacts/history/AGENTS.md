# History Mode

- **History is a mode of the panel** (ruling 76, the browser half; `src/lib/components/artifacts/history/`). The header's history button (`ArtifactPanelHeader.svelte`: the latest moment's phrase from `history/moment-text.ts`, never a number; `data-testid="artifact-history-button"`, with `artifact-version-pill` kept on a wrapper for one release) turns `DocumentWorkspace.svelte`'s `.workspace-stage` into History: a slot (`historySlot`) laid over the open body, which stays mounted underneath, `inert` and out of sight, so the editor's scroll and selection are there when History is left. The mode is ONE lazy entry, `history/history-parts.ts` (never a static import: the chat route's first load carries only `history/history-previews.ts`, whose `HISTORY_PREVIEWS` registry is the per-kind preview table and `kindHasHistory` the one test of "has a History mode"; a kind adds one loader and one component taking `HistoryPreviewProps`). The three previews: the Document's (`DocumentVersionPreview.svelte`: Markdown blocks and a word diff); the board's (`canvas/CanvasVersionPreview.svelte`, the lazy entry `canvas/history-parts.ts`: the editor's own `CanvasBoard` in its `preview` mode, with no toolbar and nothing to pick, and `HistoryDiffLayer.svelte` drawing `buildHistoryView`'s rings, New/Moved tags, trails and ghosts in board space from `diffBoards`, the camera fitted to what changed until the reader moves it); the App's (`app/AppVersionPreview.svelte`: Preview runs the version in `AppFrame` with `previewVersionId`, Code marks the lines a version added and removed with `history/line-diff.ts`). Every preview fetches stored bodies through `history/version-bodies.ts`, one small shared memory. **An App preview is read-only in the PARENT**: the version route's bridge makes the frame's own `set` reject, but an old version is code that can replace that bridge and post a raw `alfy.storage` set, so `AppFrame` refuses every `set` while `previewVersionId` is named (before anything is queued, no reply), and a 422 `not_runnable` from the route is "Code only". Entering History first awaits the open body's registered `flush` (bounded) so History starts from what is stored; leaving after a restore calls its `reload`. `HistoryMode.svelte` loads and pages (`fetchArtifactHistory`), selects, announces and lays out (side by side at 720 px and up, the rail alone with the preview pushed over it in a narrower panel, a `DialogShell` sheet on a phone); `MomentRail.svelte` is the single-select listbox whose model (`rail-model.ts`: day groups, folded steps, the key moves, merging a fresh first page) and words (`moment-text.ts`: every headline, detail, time and status in English and Hungarian from the shared `ArtifactMoment` facts, never server text) are pure and unit-tested; `MomentPreviewToolbar.svelte` holds the compare switch, the change stepper, Name and the inline Restore question. Restore exists only in the preview, and its `expectVersion` and `expectHash` are the newest version and the newest row's body hash the QUESTION was asked over (taken when it opens, never read at the moment of confirming: a version that lands meanwhile, a chat turn of Alfy's in this very tab, or a save of the reader's own that coalesced into the newest version, turns the open question into "It changed while you were looking" and guards the next press with what the rail then shows). A version an App can no longer run (`not_runnable`, below) is Code only and has no Restore, and none is offered until the preview's runnable check has answered (`HistoryPreviewState.checking`). A restore of an App writes the metadata of the body it brings back (today's audit's glitch hints, no verdict: `appMetadataAfterRestore`). On a touch screen History's hint leaves out the keyboard sentence (`isTouchDevice`). After a write History lands on the entry that holds the newest row (`newestEntryKey`: the step of a fold, not its oldest row) and says Now once (`entryIsNow`). On a phone the sheet's title steps aside while a preview is pushed, so History is named once, by the back link. A board's preview is inert where it would run something: an App block draws its poster or a card (`CanvasBoardContext.preview`, read by `AppNode.svelte`), never the live App with its writing bridge. Do not print a version number outside the toolbar's fine print (the Knowledge table's Version cell for an artifact row says the latest moment, `deriveArtifactMoment` and `latestMomentPhrase`), add a second Keep/Undo, or open a version in the editor.
