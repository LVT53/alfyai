# Take Cards

The one card a take makes, whichever kind it makes: a picture (`image`) or a clip (`video`). Parent rules: `../AGENTS.md`.

- **One shell, two bodies.** `TakeCardShell.svelte` is the card under `ArtifactCard`'s head: the stage reserved from the take's shape, the layers stacked in it (gone, the result, the shimmer, the state box with Try again), the sr-only status, the actions row (Another, Stop in the shared slot, "…") and the More menu. `image/ImageCardBody.svelte` and `video/VideoCardBody.svelte` draw only what is theirs, through its snippets: `result` (the picture, the player), `stage` (chips over the stage), `below` (a takes strip, "Made from"), `swap` (Use this, after Stop in one grid cell) and `menu` (the rows). A change to the stage, the shimmer, the state box, the actions row or the menu is made here, once: this is the one-shell waiver of `docs/plans/ltx-video/implementation-plan.md`, paid.
- **One view model.** `take-card-view.ts` holds the take's state (`takeCardState(take, now, kind, landed)`: a succeeded take whose result cannot be read is a failure with a way out, never an empty box; a kind's codes are `<kind>_blocked`, `<kind>_unchecked`, `<kind>_server_unreachable`, `<kind>_server_not_configured`), its words (`takeStateWords`, `goneWords`, `waitingLine`) and the countdown. `image/image-card-view.ts` and `video/video-card-view.ts` keep only what is the kind's: which takes a picture card holds and shows, a clip's length and file URLs, each kind's actions.
- **The time is the head's** (option C, the owner's call of 17 Nov). The shell keeps the countdown and hands its words up through `onTime`; `ArtifactCard` shows them beside the kind. Each poll's `etaSeconds` re-aims it, it ticks every `COUNTDOWN_TICK_MS`, it never moves later unless a reading is later by more than `ETA_RAISE_TOLERANCE_MS` (the server started over), and it eases an earlier reading in at no more than one extra second a second. While a take is on its way the box holds only the shimmer: the state line is the sr-only live `<kind>-card-status`, the time the sr-only, not live, `<kind>-card-status-detail`, so a poll never re-announces it.
- **Words.** What every card says is `artifacts.take.*`: the queue line, the countdown, a failure, Stop, Try again, Another, More, Download, History, the "that didn't work" toast and "open it from the chat it was made in". A kind's own sentences ("Making the picture…", "This video was blocked.") are `artifacts.image.card.*` and `artifacts.video.card.*`, chosen through `KIND_WORDS`. Both languages, always; Hungarian Try again is "Újrapróbálom".
- **Test ids carry the kind** (`image-card-stop`, `video-card-stop`), so specs tell the two cards apart. Classes are the shell's (`take-card-*`, `take-shimmer*`), global in `take-card.css`: a body draws its buttons, chips and menu rows inside the shell's slots, and the menu opens in a popover outside the card. A takes strip's tile on its way and the panel's read before a clip arrives wear the same `take-shimmer`.
- **The panel's bodies** read their item through `item-reader.svelte.ts` (`createItemReader`): the first read, then again whenever the page hears of a newer version (F23), one read at a time; a failed read or a body it cannot parse leaves what is shown.
- **Motion.** Only transform and opacity move, and hover feedback is instant. The stage never changes height between states: its layers crossfade in one grid cell.
