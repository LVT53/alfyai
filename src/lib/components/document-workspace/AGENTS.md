# Document Workspace

## One Shell For Every Document

Do:

- use `document-workspace/DocumentWorkspace.svelte` plus route-owned state for in-chat document review; do not move active-document selection into generated-file rows or `DocumentPreviewRenderer.svelte`
- use `DocumentWorkspace.svelte` as the single shell for generated files, chat attachments, library opens, and search-result opens; do not reintroduce separate modal viewers for those surfaces
- keep the shared rich-preview stack lazy-loaded from `document-workspace/DocumentWorkspace.svelte`, `FileProductionCard.svelte`, and `document-workspace/DocumentPreviewRenderer.svelte`; do not static-import the heavy preview path back into the idle chat or knowledge shell

## The Panel

- The panel is [`document-workspace/DocumentWorkspace.svelte`](DocumentWorkspace.svelte) itself, rebuilt in place — ruling 10 forbids a rename. Its content area dispatches on an item's optional `kind` through the body registry; its `list`/`onListOpenChange` props carry "what this chat made".
- **The docked panel is a column whose width moves, and the chat follows it** (FB-3, ruling 77: F3, F4, F19, F26). The panel is a flex item of the chat page's stage (`.chat-stage`) and the chat column takes what it leaves, so opening, closing, expanding, deleting and reloading change one thing, the column's width, on one curve: 0 to its width on `--ease-emphasis` over `MOTION_DURATION.emphasis` when it opens, back on `--ease-in` over `standard` when it closes, instant under reduced motion (the app's global rule for CSS, `prefersReducedMotion` for the WAAPI calls). [`src/lib/client/workspace-dock.ts`](../../client/workspace-dock.ts) is the one place that says how wide: what the panel asks for (the reader's last drag, 950 by default), never more than 68% of the stage, never leaving the chat column under `CHAT_COLUMN_FLOOR` (364 px, a composer of 340), never narrower than 440. The browser resolves it (`dockedWidthCss`, a CSS `min()`/`max()` in `--workspace-dock-width`, so the panel follows the stage in the frame the stage changes); `dockedPanelWidth` is the same number for the one thing that needs it, what the column holds, which is laid out at the width the column is growing to and shown from the right edge (`dockContentWidth`), so the editor and the board are never re-flowed through every width on the way. **Do not animate the content's width, and do not give the column a `max-width` or `min-width` that is not part of that expression**: a limit the transition cannot see is a cut. A column on its way out keeps what it showed until it has shrunk (`lastShownDocument`, `lastShownList`, `shellPresentation`): the page empties `documents` in the frame it stops asking for the panel. **Expanded is a shape, not a state of the chat**: with `reserveDock` (the chat page) the expanded panel leaves its column standing (`.workspace-dock-spacer`), so the chat keeps its width while the panel floats over it, and expand and dock are one inset animation each (`requestExpandedPresentation`, `requestDockedPresentation`: the old rect is the first keyframe, the overlay's own insets the last). **Through either animation the content is held at the size it ends at** (`heldContentSize`: the overlay's content size, measured before the first keyframe applies, on expand; `dockContentWidth` by the slot's height on dock) and shown through the moving shell from its right edge, the docked column's own way, so the edges move and nothing in the panel is re-laid out on the way; the hold is let go once the shape has flipped, where the shape's own rule holds the same size. The X closes the panel in whatever shape it is in; Escape and a press outside step out of the expanded view to the dock (`stepOutOfExpanded`), and the dock has a button of its own. **Deleting the open item shows the list in the same synchronous step that forgets it** (`handleArtifactDeleted`), so no frame of a full-width chat; the count button brings back the item the panel was closed on if it is still in the chat's list (`lastClosedItem`), else the list. **The sidebar folds to its rail while the chat would be under the floor** (`stores/ui.ts`: `sidebarFoldedForPanel`, `setSidebarFoldNeeded`, `toggleSidebarCollapsed`): the decision is the chat page's (it knows the window, the panel's ask through `onDockPreferredChange`, and the sidebar), the fold is never persisted and never touches the reader's own `sidebarCollapsed`, and a press on the sidebar's button while it is folded opens it and keeps it open for as long as that panel stays.
- **The tab remembers a panel per chat, and each says whose it is.** [`src/lib/client/document-workspace-state.ts`](../../client/document-workspace-state.ts) is the one reader and writer of that `sessionStorage` record; a caller that restores names its chat (`WorkspaceConversation`). A chat restores its own panel whole; a chat with none takes only what belongs to it plus the library and search opens from the chat visited last, and stays closed rather than opening on a stranger; an emptied panel stays as an empty one, so a closed open is not handed on by an older chat. An incognito chat's panel never travels: another chat's load removes it from storage, another chat's save does not write it back, and no incognito chat starts with another chat's.
- **The panel's layers are browser history entries** (FB-5, ruling 77: F7). [`src/lib/client/panel-history.ts`](../../client/panel-history.ts) is the one place that writes them: the panel (the list the count button opens, or an item opened straight from a chat card) is one entry on top of the chat's own, an item opened from the list a second, and an item's History (the panel's mode, ruling 76) one more over its item (FX-2: a Back leaves History and the item stays; `PanelLayer`'s `history`, depth 2 over a card's item and 3 over a list's), and each says which layer it shows in SvelteKit's `page.state` (`App.PageState.alfyPanel`, kept by the browser across Back, Forward and a reload). The chat page wires it (`panelLayer`, `applyPanelLayer`, `createPanelHistory`): a click, the X, Escape, a press outside and the count button only change the page's flags and the history follows them (a close pops the entries it added, so the next Back leaves the chat instead of landing on a chat whose panel is already shut); a Back or Forward puts the panel where the entry says through the same functions a click uses (`openArtifactList`, `selectWorkspaceDocument`, `closeWorkspace`), never the flags. History stays the panel's to open and close (it flushes the open body and loads its own chunk): the page's wish goes down as `DocumentWorkspace`'s `historyWanted` and what happened comes back through `onHistoryOpenChange` (a press, Done, Escape, the panel closing, the list showing and another item opening all report it), and History goes with its item. On a phone History is a sheet with its own preview and list peeling (Escape, the grabber); the browser's Back leaves History as a whole. Do not write the panel's entries anywhere else, call `history.pushState` for it, or add a `popstate` listener of your own that sets the panel's state.
- **One body per open item, and what it reports to the header** (final polish D1/D2). The workspace mounts a kind's body under `{#key activeBodyKey}` — the open item's id (`artifactId ?? id`), never the `activeDocument` object — so a version change or a Keep never rebuilds the editor, while another item always gets a body of its own. What the body reports to the panel header (the Versions/Download/Comments triggers, the comment count, whether the comments show) is filed in one record stamped with that item (`bodyPanelReport`) and read back only while that item is the open one. Do not clear it from an effect keyed on the open item: the body registers once, when it mounts, and such an effect can wipe a registration made in the same flush. A live Alfy call is applied by the body that was mounted while it ran; a body built after it settled restores the server's review state (ruling 61) and never lands it (`settledActivityKeyAtMount` in `DocumentBody.svelte`) — landing it on top counted the change twice.

Do not:

- rename `DocumentWorkspace.svelte`, or fork a second viewer for an artifact kind — the panel is the only shell
