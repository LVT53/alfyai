# Drawing Kit

Small drawn copies of real controls and of the parts of a screen, composed into realistic drawings of real screens inside a `DrawingFrame`: the chat (`DrawnReply`, `DrawnUserMessage`, `DrawnComposer`, `DrawnMenu`), the side panel (`DrawnPanel`, `DrawnChatCard`), Settings (`DrawnSettingsCard`, `DrawnSettingsRow`, `DrawnSelect`, `DrawnSegmented`, `DrawnSwitch`, `DrawnButton`, `DrawnConnections`), the Knowledge Base (`DrawnSwitcher`, `DrawnChip`, `DrawnDocuments`, `DrawnMemoryProfile`), the sidebar and Home (`DrawnSidebar`, `DrawnHomeBand`, `DrawnHomeProjects`, `DrawnRecent`) and the permission card (`DrawnWriteCard`), with `DrawnBar` for content and `DrawnSpot` for a spot no part holds. The Feature Guide draws one per section, in `src/lib/components/guide/drawings/`. Campaign pictures will draw from the same parts later (`docs/plans/feature-guide/design.md`, section 9). Only drawings import this folder: nothing in the chat shell does.

## Rules

- **Words are the real control's dictionary key**, read from the component that renders the control (`$t(...)`). A reworded control then rewords its drawing, in both languages. A drawing never gets a key of its own, and content (a message, a title, a name) is a grey `DrawnBar`.
- **Icons are Lucide**, the ones the real control imports: a kind's icon is `ARTIFACT_KIND_ICONS` (the Knowledge Base's chips carry their own, which differ for a canvas). A part arranges icons and simple shapes (elements in token colours) and draws no icon of its own: an inline `svg` is out. A mark that is a brand glyph in the real control (`BrandIcon.svelte`) is drawn only where the real one falls back to a Lucide icon. An icon sits in a `.drawn-ic` box (`size="100%"`; `--ic` sets its size).
- **Tokens for colour, font and shadow.** Colours, fonts and shadows are `src/app.css` tokens, and light and dark come from them alone: no hex, no `rgb()`, no `.dark` branch. Sizes, radii and type sizes are design pixels (next rule), since a rem token cannot scale with the frame.
- **Design pixels.** A drawing is designed at 330 by 222 and `DrawingFrame` scales it to its container's width with container-query units (the frame's height is its `aspect-ratio`, so nothing is measured and nothing jumps after hydration). A part sizes itself in `calc(var(--u) * N)`, N being its size in the 330-wide design: never in `rem` (it cannot scale) and never in `px` but for a 1px border. A column may be a `%` of the frame (a drawing's two panes are). Labels must fit at any width: a part draws no ellipsis, and `nowrap` text gets room for the longer Hungarian.
- **Decorative.** The frame is `aria-hidden`, so nothing inside takes focus: spans, never buttons or links. Give the frame a container as wide as the drawing may be; it fills it.
- **Spots.** A control a guide row can light carries `data-drawing-spot="<id>"` and the class `drawing-spot-lit` while lit (`drawing-spot.ts`, `isLit`). The look is `src/app.css`'s `.spotlight-ring, .drawing-spot-lit` rule, the ring Show me puts on the real control. The drawing names the ids, which are the guide's `drawingSpot`s listed per section in `guide/drawings/section-drawing-spots.ts`; a part holding a spot takes `lit` and the ids it marks (`spots`, or `spot`). The frame positions every spot, so a part sets no `position` on one. A spot no part holds (two rows a row of the guide is about, a whole card, a region of a page) is wrapped in `DrawnSpot`: its `radius` is the corner the ring follows, and a Settings row's `bleed` lets the ring stand clear of the text, as the row's own box does. A spot may be drawn twice (a row about Memory rings the portrait and the switch). The ring reaches 6px whatever the scale and the frame clips it, so leave a spot 7 design pixels or more from the frame's edge.

## A drawing follows its control

A change to a pictured control's icon, order or place changes its part in the same change; words follow on their own, through their keys. `guide/drawings/drawings.test.ts` holds what a test can: the "+" menu's rows against `buildComposerMenuRows`, the Knowledge Base's chips against `DOCUMENT_TYPE_FILTER_ORDER`, the sidebar's sections in the order the list draws them, the exact dictionary keys each drawing reads (two controls can share a word, so the key says which one a drawing follows), the tokens and Lucide rules above. A spot added or removed changes `SECTION_DRAWING_SPOTS` too.
